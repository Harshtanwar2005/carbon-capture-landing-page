The Innovation Roadmap

How carbon capture engineering is set to evolve over the coming decades.

2030 Phase 1

Industrial Retrofitting & Scaling

Widespread adoption of post-combustion capture units across major steel, cement, and power production hubs, backed by government emission mandates and tax incentives.

2035 Phase 2

Direct Air Capture (DAC) Networks

Deployment of massive modular DAC facilities powered entirely by dedicated local solar and wind microgrids, drastically lowering the energy cost per ton of extracted CO₂.

2040 Phase 3

AI & Advanced Nanomaterials

Integration of real-time AI automation and quantum-designed synthetic filter membranes that capture carbon with near-zero chemical degradation and higher efficiency.

2050 Phase 4

Global Net-Negative Ecosystems

Transitioning from mere emission reduction to planetary-scale carbon removal, effectively stabilizing global atmospheric greenhouse gas concentrations.
