Engineering The Process

A focused technical breakdown of how industrial carbon capture moves from factory emissions to secure subterranean storage.

The Technical Workflow

Carbon capture and storage (CCS) operates through a strict multi-stage industrial engineering sequence. Every phase from capturing exhaust gases to underground injection is designed with precision to safeguard our atmospheric balance.

Step-by-Step Mechanism

Phase 01

Industrial Capture

Exhaust flue gases emerging from large power stations and heavy manufacturing units are directed through specialized chemical solvents or membrane units to selectively separate carbon dioxide before smoke exits the stacks.

Phase 02

Compression & Transport

Once isolated, the carbon dioxide is purified and subjected to intense pressure, converting it into a dense supercritical liquid state suitable for safe transit across dedicated high-pressure steel pipeline networks.

Phase 03

Permanent Storage

The compressed fluid is injected deeply beneath the Earth's crust into stable saline formations or depleted geological reservoirs, where impermeable rock layers permanently lock the emissions away from the atmosphere.
