Engineering The Process

A focused technical breakdown of how industrial carbon capture moves from factory emissions to secure subterranean storage.

Core Industrial Methods

Hover over the cards below to explore each specialized capture technique.

METHOD 01

Post-Combustion

Separates carbon dioxide from flue gases after fossil fuels are burned. Highly practical because it fits easily into existing industrial plants without a full redesign.

METHOD 02

Pre-Combustion

Converts fuel into a gas mixture before burning. The carbon is separated beforehand, making the resulting hydrogen-rich clean fuel much easier to manage.

METHOD 03

Oxy-Fuel Combustion

Burns fuel using pure oxygen instead of standard air. This generates an exhaust stream made almost entirely of CO2 and water vapor, simplifying extraction.

METHOD 04

Direct Air Capture

Deploys massive mechanical fans embedded with chemical filter systems to pull carbon dioxide directly out of the ambient atmosphere anywhere in the world.
