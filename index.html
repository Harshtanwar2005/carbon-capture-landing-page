Learn About Carbon Capture

Discover how modern industrial engineering captures and securely stores carbon emissions.

What Is Carbon Capture?

Carbon capture is a process that collects carbon dioxide from industrial sources before it enters the atmosphere.

Carbon Storage

Captured carbon dioxide can be transported and stored safely in suitable underground geological formations.

Why Is Carbon Capture Important?

Carbon dioxide is released into the atmosphere by many industrial activities. Carbon capture is one of the most vital pathways to reduce these emissions. It assists heavy industries in managing their carbon footprint and supports global efforts to combat climate change.

How Does It Work?

01

Capture

Carbon dioxide is efficiently separated from exhaust gases produced by large factories and power plants.

02

Transport

The captured carbon dioxide is compressed and moved securely through specialized pipelines or carriers.

03

Storage

Finally, carbon dioxide is injected deep underground into suitable rock formations for permanent storage.
